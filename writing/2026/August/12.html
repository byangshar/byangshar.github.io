The March Against The Establishment

There is a way, 
they used to say 
as they made us march ahead
to risk our lives for them.

Groups on groups 
led to the ground,
packs of cigarettes thrown around, 
and the terror of the sound
of people shouting and crying about,
but mere indistinct chatter
here at home,
where all sound and smoke blurs into one whole.

In the months of war, 
God walked off 
and ascended from the clouds
to a much higher ground,
forgoing again when duty called, 
indulging in all things
we’re advised against, 
and are told certain refrains keep us sane.

And the one sin of man: 
to seek all escapes,
and passage of ways through time,
and wander about the unsought path.
Here people are found hung all around,
debt weighing their chests and arms, 
others with a tinge of pain, from reclusion and refrain,
and calamities of all sorts.
Some infants, some of age, 
some that awaited promises once made, 
some with a hundred letters to their chest,
some with a somber, chilling death.
All of them nailed or hung to their tree, 
with their flowers all around,
a sun that never went down.
Seldom clouds, seldom shade,
seldom cold, but never rain;
nothing to tear the flesh,
no bones for the vultures at rest.
No guards, no theft,
the trail went on
until a suitable tree was met, 
and then it consumed all remaining life,
and embraced them, until whatever end.

The nobles ostracised all that went on the trail, 
if returned, they were jailed,
then made to starve to death.
Never finding the tree that was meant for them, 
and the rats nibbled on their flesh,
until their bones were thrown to the dogs in the lawn, 
that could’ve lived off the pigs at rest,
yet due to the hives,
all lives are lost, all lives are lost,
all lives are lost…

There is a way,
there always has been,
an eye peeks from a hole in the seam,
and once apathy is left aside,
this is the spirit of revolt.
This is when we aim back at the lords
and shoot, and claim the estate, 
then foster a state safe for all that stay,
in peace, amongst all, or in seclusion for a month—
this is how art was made.

Now there ought to be fewer promises undelivered
when one walks further along the path, 
fewer people with no letters of love,
and discolouration, and wilted flowers.
The sun is still overhead, 
the plague was always amongst us,
as we looked into the sky for cloudshed,
it dug into the ground, and built estates all around.
